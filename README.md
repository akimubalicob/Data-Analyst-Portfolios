# 📊 Serdi's Data Analyst Portfolio

Welcome to my Data Analyst Portfolio! This repository showcases a selection of hands-on projects that reflect my ability to solve real-world business problems using data. Each notebook demonstrates a different analytical approach—from predictive modeling and customer segmentation to statistical hypothesis testing. These projects were designed to simulate professional scenarios where data-driven decisions are critical to business success.

As a data analyst, I focus on translating operational needs into actionable insights, building reliable models, and communicating results clearly to stakeholders. Whether it's helping HR predict employee attrition, guiding a bank's marketing strategy, or supporting a product team with statistical evidence, my goal is to deliver value through data.

**Highlights:**

- Performed a two-sample t-test to compare user groups.
- Interpreted results for leadership decision-making.
- Collaborated with cross-functional team members.
